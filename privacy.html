OpenClaw Backups

Privacy policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026.

Purpose and data

This personal utility stores encrypted backup archives in the owner's Google Drive. It uses the Google Drive API to create, list, upload, download, verify, and manage its backup files, and to check storage availability.

The requested permission is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It covers files created by, or explicitly made available to, this application. The utility does not request access to Gmail, contacts, or the entire Drive.

Storage and security

Backup contents may include personal workspace files, agent memory, conversation history, settings, and saved authentication state. These contents are encrypted on the owner's server before transmission to Google Drive. The private recovery key is kept separately and is not uploaded with the archive.

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored in a protected configuration on the owner's server to permit scheduled backups. Backup filenames, timestamps, sizes, and checksums remain visible to Google Drive. Data travels over HTTPS.

Use and sharing

Google account access is used solely to operate these personal backups. This utility does not sell data, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train AI models. Backup files remain in the owner's Drive under its existing access controls. Google processes stored files under its own applicable terms and policies.

Retention and control

The configured retention keeps the newest completed backup from each of seven dates and four calendar weeks, with overlapping copies counted once. Older eligible backups are moved to Drive Trash. Items in Trash may continue to occupy storage until removed under Google's retention rules or by the owner.

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account settings, stop the scheduled job on the server, or delete the backup files in Drive. Revoking access stops future API operations but does not itself delete existing backups.

Contact

For questions, use the support email displayed on this application's Google authorization screen.
