OpenClaw Backups

A personal backup utility that copies encrypted OpenClaw data archives from the owner's server to the owner's Google Drive.

It uses Google Drive's drive.file permission to create and manage its backup files. Google account authorization is required. It is intended for the owner's personal use and is not a public file-storage service.

The backup data can include workspace files, memory, conversations, settings, and authentication state. Archives are encrypted before upload. The private recovery key is kept separately from Google Drive.

Privacy policy

Support: use the support email displayed on this application's Google authorization screen.

This utility is independently configured by its owner. It is not an official Google or OpenClaw service.
